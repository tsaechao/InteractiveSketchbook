milkytrinh

milkytrinh is known for her creations in various mediums, but most notably her work with ceramics as the one displayed above. milkytrinh is an artist that shows how anything could be turned into art and inspires me to continously experiment and try new mediums, even outside the norm.


pearfleur

pearfleur is an artist that primarily dabbles in watercolors and goauche as well as her soothing, relaxing vlogs that show her entire art process. similar to milkytrinh, pearfleur shows the various ways in which mediums can be used, as well as her choice of bold, vibrant use of primary colors.


frannerd/fran meneses

frannerd, otherwise known as fran meneses, is a NYC based illustrator and content creator. She's most known for her short comics featuring every day life drawn in her distinct style and cool, pastel color palette. She is an artist that inspires me as her strict color palette makes her art more interesting to me and really captures my attention.
