checkpoint two

assessment two: october 26

For last Monday's activity, the person who peer reviewed my site was Khai Bui. They left a lot of positive and great notes, with little to no notes on what should be improved besides my navigation which is what I did. They made a point about how the colors chosen for my navigation bar were a bit too light and could be hard for some to see, suggesting that I make it bolder to stand out a bit more, so I adjusted both the colors of the links and the color of the visited links so they are a bit darker down and hopefully more readible while maintaining the original muted pastel look I was going for. They also made notes about the hierarchy on my inspo page so I also went ahead and adjusted the title a bit where my name is so it's more visible. I appreaciated the comments left by my peer reviewer and because I was placed in a group with two other classmates, after looking at both of their sites, I liked how they organized their projects into a separate page instead of listing is all in the navigation bar. Taking inspiration from both of them, my projects are now all listed in their own page rather than taking up too much space in the navigation bar to account for the growing amount of assignments we have. Taking this same idea, I chose to do the same for my checkpoints as well as creating another page for all of my moved reflections that were previously on other pages. I feel that this definitely helps with navigation making things a lot simpler rather than just listing everything in the navigation bar which started to look more cluttered. Though I still have projects to catch up on, I now have a new space to place all of my projects without having to change every other page to add a new page to the nav bar.
