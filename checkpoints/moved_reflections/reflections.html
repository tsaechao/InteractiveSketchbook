landing page

The article "The Difference Between Information Architecture (IA) and Navigation" stresses the importance and difference between information architecture and navigation in websites. While IA is the "backbone" of the site and navigation is what the user sees to reach the specific page they're looking for, I feel like I've done both in this assignment, organizing all information within VSC and also creating a navigation for all the previous pages made before. Though I followed the exact example provided in the online lecture, I did think it would be best to have the navigation at the very top where it's easy to see for the user to get from page to page. As for organizing the pages, I again just followed what was shown in the videos provided to avoid any confusion of my own. The most difficult part was figuring out how to tweak each page's navigation so it would link to the correct page and not sure why but renaming everything was somehow difficult for me as well. The sketch of course is just an idea so I didn't actually expect to build what I drew, it's more so practice and a learning process in my eyes since this is only a rough draft and does feel temporary and believe that there's still a lot that I need to learn about CSS and VSC before figuring out what to change.


about page

In all honesty, I was really procrastinating this assignment as it seemed too daunting but now that I've actually done it, it was genuinely super enjoyable to do. What really helped was having creative freedom with the video self-portrait, while I don’t particularly mind filming a video of myself, it’d be preferable if I could also avoid it as much as possible. It often feels weird, clunky and in a way disingenuous, like you’re putting on a mask to look more open. Chiikawa, the plushy featured in the video, is a character that I’ve been obsessed with ever since it popped up on my feed about four years ago and something I feel represents me as many friends and family members have often told me that they think of me when they see it. One thing that I decided to leave out of my biography, because I thought it was unnecessary, was the reason why I switched to VisCom and dropped Pre-Nursing. I couldn’t really think of a way to summarize it and not sound obnoxious so I ultimately decided to just drop that part. Overall, I think there could be some improvements made to my page but still pretty satisfied with the end result.
