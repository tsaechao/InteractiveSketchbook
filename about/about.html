about me

Tessa Saechao

Hello I’m Tessa! I’m currently nineteen, turning twenty in a little less than two weeks from now and just recently became a Visual Communication Design major this semester after two years of Pre-Nursing at SFSU. Born and raised in California, more specifically in Contra Costa County, I’ve spent almost all my life in the suburbs of Oakley until three years ago, moving to a neighboring city known as Antioch. Like many people in VisCom, I grew up making art, for me it was usually water-based mediums, such as watercolors and gouache, but eventually fell out of practice. It wouldn’t be until Freshman year of high school that graphic design would come into my life. While I thought I’d only stick around for so long like many other art classes, it became something that I eventually grew to love, however not so much Adobe platforms… It was a new form of expression that wasn’t traditional art or digital painting/drawing, almost like a whole new language and it was something that was more flexible and easily accessible for me to do.


I hope to “reconnect” with my roots in a way and learn so many new things throughout my journey through VisCom, and particularly excited for this class as I hope to work in web design in the future!
